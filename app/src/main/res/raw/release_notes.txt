OpenRide 1.2: Seven new mini-games, Import Peloton workout history, Delete a ride, Improvements to video playback, Bug fixes

New in 1.2:

Seven new games: Watt Lane, Balloon Pump, Keepy Uppy, Power Grid, Ready for Launch, The Pull and Gate Crasher.

Fresh circuits: Each part of a circuit now picks from a pool of games that suit it, so the same circuit plays differently every ride. The new games ask for about a third of your FTP to reach their goal; the harder you push the more stars you earn.

Mute Classes: Classes now let you ride to your own music, and a class opened while music is playing starts muted. Classes also ask YouTube for a format the bike plays smoothly, so the picture stays sharp and does not stutter.

History Import: You can now delete a ride from its details screen, and import your past rides from Peloton's workout-history CSV in Profile. Thanks to coyotemojo and Drewmiller1 for the PR.
